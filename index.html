The Calibration Law: Diagnosing and Repairing Answer-Position Bias in Mixture-of-Experts Vision-Language Models

Indra Imanuel, et al.

Manuscript in preparation (ICML 2027 target) · independent project, Jan 2026 – present

MoE vision–language models fail badly at telling apart images that differ in one small detail. We show the underlying failure is a severe answer-position bias, introduce a zero-cost diagnostic for it (the bias index), and find, across eight open MoE VLMs, that training-free interventions succeed in proportion to how well they calibrate that bias. A label-free recipe substantially improves the VisMin group score and transfers zero-shot to Winoground and ColorSwap.

When Can a Second Pass Fix Document Structure? An End-to-End Anatomy of Hierarchy Repair

Indra Imanuel, et al.

Manuscript in preparation (ICDAR 2027 target) · Laputa, 2026

Can a cheap second pass repair the document hierarchy that PDF extractors get wrong? A self-supervised 115M pointer model trained without human labels, benchmarked end-to-end against rules, a 26B LLM, and the supervised 4B multimodal state of the art on three benchmarks. Preliminary results show deep, unnumbered book hierarchy is largely invisible to text-side and crop-level methods alike.

Optimized Color Filter Array for Denoising Diffusion Null-Space Model-Based Demosaicing

Indra Imanuel, Hyoseon Yang, Suk-Ho Lee

IEEE Access, 2024 SCIE · Ph.D. thesis work

A color-filter-array pattern and optimization scheme designed jointly with a diffusion null-space demosaicing model, giving better demosaiced images and compression rates than fixed patterns.

Super-resolution with adversarial loss on the feature maps of the generated high-resolution image

Indra Imanuel, Suk-Ho Lee

Electronics Letters, 2022 SCIE · M.Sc. thesis work

A GAN training scheme that applies the adversarial loss on the feature maps of the generated high-resolution image, via pre-trained networks, instead of on pixels alone. It improved on prior GAN-based super-resolution and outperformed the state-of-the-art CVPR method on a real-world low-resolution benchmark at the time of publication.
