Independent project · Jan 2026 – present

Training-free diagnosis and repair of fine-grained discrimination failures in eight open MoE VLMs. A zero-cost diagnostic (the bias index), a "calibration law" for interventions, and causal localization of the repair to final-layer prefill routing.
