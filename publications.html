The Calibration Law: Diagnosing and Repairing Answer-Position Bias in Mixture-of-Experts Vision-Language Models

Indra Imanuel, et al.

In preparation for ICML 2027 · independent project; I proposed and lead the project

MoE vision–language models fail badly at fine-grained visual discrimination. On the VisMin benchmark we show the underlying failure is a severe answer-position bias, introduce a zero-cost diagnostic (the bias index) that tracks capability across eight open MoE VLMs, and establish a "calibration law": training-free interventions — router steering, prompting, output-logit calibration — succeed in proportion to how well they calibrate the bias. A label-free recipe greatly improves the overall VisMin group score and transfers zero-shot to Winoground and ColorSwap; causal analysis localizes the repair to final-layer prefill routing.

When Can a Second Pass Fix Document Structure? An End-to-End Anatomy of Hierarchy Repair

Indra Imanuel, et al.

In preparation for ICDAR 2027 · Laputa, Inc.; I proposed and lead the project

PDF extractors recover text well and document hierarchy poorly. We ask whether a cheap second pass can repair it: a self-supervised 115M pointer model trained on pseudo-labels mined from section numbering and embedded PDF outlines — no human annotation — evaluated end-to-end against deterministic rules, a 26B LLM, and the supervised 4B multimodal state of the art on three benchmarks. Preliminary results show that deep, unnumbered book hierarchy is largely invisible to text-side and crop-level methods alike, and point to cheap structural signals as the more promising route than larger models.

Super-resolution with adversarial loss on the feature maps of the generated high-resolution image

Indra Imanuel, Suk-Ho Lee

Electronics Letters, vol. 58, pp. 47–49, 2022 SCIE, Scopus Q2 · M.Sc. thesis work

A GAN training scheme that applies adversarial loss on feature maps of the generated high-resolution image via pre-trained networks; outperformed the state-of-the-art CVPR method on a real-world low-resolution benchmark at the time of publication.
